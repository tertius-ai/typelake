# Interesting strong Steiner categories

This is the editorial register for the TypeBase Volume I “interesting” page.
This register is a manual editorial guide. The builder selects recognized families, nontrivial homotopy-Poset cases, and four explicit small labels after constructing the complete label map; it does not parse this Markdown register.

## Initial cases

- The empty category and the point mark the two boundary conventions.
- The walking arrow is the Gray unit test and the first category with a
  directed cell.
- The parallel pair and the composable pair distinguish multiplicity from
  path composition in dimension one.
- The 2-globe has two parallel generating 1-cells and one generating 2-cell.
- The 2-oriental has a 2-cell from its long edge to the composite of its two
  short edges; its 0-to-2 mapping category is the walking arrow.
- The 3-globe is the first three-dimensional pilot object.
- The directed square, D^1 tensor D^1, is the first genuinely two-variable
  Gray product. It enters the complete size-9 edition.
- The first categories with a non-singleton homotopy Poset demonstrate why
  the Gepner--Heine data is not reducible to the atomic boundary table.
- `10.2.51` has four parallel arrows `a0,...,a3` from `v0` to `v1` and four
  generating 2-cells `a0 => a2`, `a0 => a3`, `a1 => a2`, and `a1 => a3`.
  Its fundamental Poset at the oriented boundary `v0 -> v1` is the four-point
  crown `4.1.7`; its order complex is a circle and `H_1 = Z`. The same local
  Poset occurs in `11.2.85` and `11.2.270`.
- `11.2.165` has fundamental Poset `5.1.10` at `v0 -> v1`: a four-point crown
  together with an isolated point. It has two components and still has
  `H_1 = Z`.
- The first nontrivial automorphism action and the first non-self-dual pair
  provide symmetry and duality cases for the catalogue.

Named selection is curated, while the projection builder also includes the exact
nontrivial-core criterion recorded in `PLAN.md`. Neither is a numerical
“interestingness” score, and being listed does not enlarge the declared
complete coverage region.
