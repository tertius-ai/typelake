# Data licence and community norms

**Published data is CC0 1.0** — see [`LICENSE-DATA`](LICENSE-DATA). Code is MIT,
see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

CC0 places the data in the public domain worldwide, waiving copyright and the EU
*sui generis* database right. **You owe us nothing legally.** No attribution
clause, no share-alike, no field-of-use limit, nothing for an institutional
lawyer to review before you build on it.

## Why CC0

Most of what we publish — discriminants, homology groups, ring presentations —
consists of *facts*, which in the United States are not copyrightable at all
(*Feist*, 1991). Licensing them under an attribution clause would assert rights
that largely do not exist, leaving users unable to tell which parts actually
bind. Cleanly waiving is more honest than vaguely reserving.

The practical reason is attribution stacking. Because provenance here is recorded
**per value**, a single volume can carry thousands of distinct contributors; an
attribution clause would make lawful reuse of a combined dataset nearly
impossible to comply with. That cost falls hardest on exactly the downstream
research we want to enable.

## What we ask instead

These are norms, not licence terms. Nothing below is legally binding.

- **Cite the volume**, by its version DOI when you mean a specific release, or
  its concept DOI when you mean the series.
- **Cite objects by label** when a claim rests on particular ones.
- **Record the snapshot id** if you queried the lake rather than a frozen volume,
  since the lake grows and your result should stay reproducible.
- **Say what you checked.** Every value carries a rigor level and its
  assumptions; a GRH-conditional class number should not be reported as
  unconditional because the qualification was inconvenient.
- **Report errors.** Corrections become errata attached to the volume, so a
  finding of yours reaches everyone who reads it afterwards.

## Contributing

**Submitted facts are dedicated CC0**, on the same terms as everything else — a
contribution cannot carry an attribution obligation into a public-domain store.
Your `submitter` is recorded in the provenance of every fact you contribute and
is published with it, so credit is preserved as data even though it is not
compelled as a licence term.

If your institution cannot make a public-domain dedication, tell us before
submitting rather than after.
